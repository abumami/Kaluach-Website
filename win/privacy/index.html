Privacy Policy · Kaluach™ for Windows

Available in the Microsoft Store

Kaluach™ for Windows collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app sends nothing over the internet. (A link you click, such as to this website, opens in your browser.)

Where your data is kept

Your events, saved locations, custom zmanim shitot and settings are stored on your PC, in your Windows user folder. They are not synced anywhere, and the developer never receives them. A file you export (events, or a year’s CSV) goes only where you save it.

Location

Kaluach for Windows doesn’t use your PC’s location. You choose a place by searching its built-in list of cities or by typing coordinates; the search happens on your PC and nothing is sent anywhere.

Reminders

Event reminders are scheduled with Windows on your PC and shown as Windows notifications. Nothing is sent to a server.

Installing and updates

If you install Kaluach for Windows from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the download, licence and updates under the Microsoft Privacy Statement; the developer receives only the anonymous totals the Store reports to every developer (such as the number of installs), and, if you let Windows send diagnostic data, Microsoft’s reports of the app’s crashes.

Error log

If the app stops unexpectedly, a short error log is written on your PC so that a problem can be diagnosed. It is never sent anywhere; you can choose to include it in a support message.

Contact form

If you write through the support page, the name, email address and message you enter are delivered to the developer by the form service Web3Forms and used only to answer you.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
