Kaluach for Android

In development · testers wanted for Google Play

The same calendars, zmanim and personal events as Kaluach for the Mac and iKaluach, on Android phones.

What it does

  • Civil Month and Hebrew Month views, Today and Go to Year.
  • Jewish, Israeli and civil holidays, the weekly parsha and special Shabbatot, for Israel or the diaspora.
  • Zmanim for any location, by the built-in shitot or your own.
  • Personal events — azkarot, birthdays, anniversaries, one-time dates — with reminders.
  • Zmanim alarms, never on Shabbat or Yom Tov.
  • Print a year of Shabbatot and holidays, or export every day to a CSV spreadsheet.
  • English and Hebrew. Move your events to and from Kaluach on the Mac, iPhone and Windows with the Kaluach events file.

Requirements

Android 8.0 or later.

Become a tester

Kaluach for Android is almost ready for Google Play. Before a new app can be published, Google requires a closed test: at least 12 people using it for 14 days in a row. You can help — and have Kaluach on your phone before anyone else.

What you need

  • An Android phone or tablet with Android 8.0 or later.
  • A Google account — the one you use for Google Play on that phone.

What we ask

  • Keep Kaluach installed for at least two weeks.
  • Open it now and then: check the zmanim, look up a date, add a yahrzeit or a birthday.
  • Tell us what you find — anything wrong, confusing or missing.

How to join

  1. Join the testers group: Kaluach Android testers. Sign in with the same Google account as on your phone.
  2. When the test opens, we’ll post the testing link here and send it to the group. Open it on your phone and tap Become a tester.
  3. Install Kaluach from Google Play, from the link on that page.

Your email address is used only to give you access to the test, and members of the group can’t see each other. To stop testing, leave the group and uninstall Kaluach.
