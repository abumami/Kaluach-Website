Privacy Policy · Kaluach for Android

In development · coming later to Google Play

Kaluach for Android collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Where your data is kept

Your events, saved locations, custom zmanim shitot, alarm settings and preferences are stored on your phone. The developer never receives them. A file you export (events, or a year’s CSV) goes only where you save or share it.

Android backup

If backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include the app’s data in your own backup in your Google account, so that it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; the developer has no access to it.

Location

Location is optional. If you choose Use My Location, the app asks for your approximate location and uses it on the phone to calculate zmanim. To show the place’s name, and when you search for a city, the app uses your phone’s place-search service (on most phones, provided by Google), which receives the text you type or the approximate position under its own privacy policy. The developer does not receive these requests.

Alarms and reminders

Zmanim alarms and event reminders are scheduled on the phone itself. Nothing is sent to a server. The app asks permission to show notifications, and to set its alarms again after the phone restarts.

Contact form

If you write through the support page, the name, email address and message you enter are delivered to the developer by the form service Web3Forms and used only to answer you.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
