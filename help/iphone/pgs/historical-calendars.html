Julian and Gregorian Calendar Transitions

Historical and genealogical records often use the civil calendar that was actually in force in their region.

In Settings, choose the transition convention iKaluach should use when displaying historical civil dates:

SettingFirst Gregorian date
Papal (default)15 October 1582
United Kingdom14 September 1752
Russian14 February 1918

Before the selected transition, iKaluach displays the historically appropriate Julian civil date wherever civil dates appear in the app.

This is a display convention. It does not change zmanim, Hebrew-calendar navigation, or the way events are calculated or stored. After locating a historical day, create its event normally; the event retains its Hebrew date.
