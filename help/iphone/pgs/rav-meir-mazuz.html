Zmanim According to Rav Meir Mazuz and Kisei Rachamim

This method reflects the Tunisian tradition’s attention to exact reading, inherited measures, and astronomical precision.

The rav and his community

Rav Meir Nissim Mazuz was born in Tunis in 1945. His father, Rav Matzliach Mazuz, founded Yeshivat Kisei Rachamim to preserve the rigorous Tunisian method of learning. After his father was murdered in 1971, Rav Meir and his brothers rebuilt the yeshiva in Bnei Brak. Rav Mazuz died in 2025.

The method’s character

Alot and Tzeit follow the sun’s actual position below the horizon. Misheyakir and Rabbeinu Tam use fixed minutes, while afternoon calculations return to the sunrise-to-sunset day.

ZmanPlain-language rule
AlotSun 16.11 degrees below the horizon
Misheyakir60 fixed minutes before sunrise; 66 in pressing need
Mincha Ketana9.5 daylight proportional hours after sunrise
Plag1.25 daylight proportional hours before sunset
TzeitSun 4.85 degrees below the horizon
Rabbeinu Tam72 fixed minutes after sunset
