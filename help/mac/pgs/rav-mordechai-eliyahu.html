Zmanim According to Rav Mordechai Eliyahu

This method carries a Jerusalem-Baghdad inheritance shaped by the Ben Ish Chai tradition.

The rav and his community

Rav Mordechai Eliyahu was born in Jerusalem in 1929 to a family rooted in Baghdad’s rabbinic and kabbalistic world. He became a dayan and later Rishon LeZion. His teaching joined modern Israeli questions with the customs of Jerusalem and Iraqi-Sephardi practice.

The method’s character

The morning begins with a proportional Alot based on daylight. The extended day used for several deadlines ends at a practical Ben Ish Chai endpoint after sunset. Different evening times may serve different halachic purposes.

ZmanPlain-language rule
AlotOne eighth of daylight before sunrise, equivalent to 90 proportional minutes
MisheyakirAbout one proportional hour before sunrise
Shema and TefillahMeasured in the extended day from Alot to the calculation Tzeit
ChatzotSolar noon
Mincha Gedolah30 fixed minutes after Chatzot
Calculation Tzeit20 fixed minutes after sunset
