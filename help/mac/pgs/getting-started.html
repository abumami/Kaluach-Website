Kaluach Mac Help

Getting Started

Kaluach places the civil and Hebrew calendars side by side, with holidays, Torah portions, zmanim for the selected location, and personal events such as yahrzeits, birthdays, and anniversaries.

The main window

The calendar fills the window, and the Details pane on its right shows everything about the selected day. The pane is open when Kaluach first starts; the panel button at the right end of the toolbar shows or hides it.

Choose your location

Kaluach starts in Jerusalem, with Pittsburgh also in Saved Locations — one place in Israel and one in the diaspora. Use the location control in the toolbar to search for a city, use the Mac’s current location, or enter coordinates manually.

The location determines the astronomical data, whether calendar behavior follows Israel or the diaspora, and the local candle-lighting offset. It is saved automatically, and saved locations remain available for quick reselection.

Finding something in this guide

Help → Kaluach Mac Help opens this guide in its own window. Type a word or two in the search field at the top right: the pages that contain them are listed — click one to open it. The search follows the app’s language, English or Hebrew.
