Privacy Policy

Kaluach™ Mac and iKaluach™ collect no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your events, saved locations, custom zmanim methods, alarm settings and preferences are stored only on your Mac or iPhone (and in your device’s own backups). The developer never receives them.

Location

Location is optional. If you choose Use My Location, your position is used on the device to calculate zmanim. To show the place’s name and time zone, the app asks Apple’s map service, as Apple’s own apps do. When you search for a city, the text you type is sent to Apple’s map service to find matching places. These requests are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy; the developer does not receive them.

Alarms and reminders

Zmanim alarms and event reminders are scheduled on the device itself. Nothing is sent to a server.

Contact form

If you write through the support page, the name, email address and message you enter are delivered to the developer by the form service Web3Forms and used only to answer you.

Children

The apps collect no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
